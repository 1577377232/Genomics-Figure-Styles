# Literature and style rationale

The palettes are independently developed and **inspired by the visual organization**, not exact color codes or artwork, of related published pan-genome, TE, and SV genetics research.

- Nature Genetics (2024): *Structural variation reshapes population gene expression and trait variation in 2,105 Brassica napus accessions*. Relevant to multigroup SV/genomic plot structure. Verify exact figure and metadata in the original publication before citing in a manuscript.
- Nature Communications (2026): *Phased-assembly-driven pangenome graphs for structural variant genotyping and complex trait mapping in dairy cattle*. Relevant to mixed SNP/SV GWAS.
- Advanced Science (2026): *A Pan-TE Map Reveals the Important Role of Transposable Elements in Gene Expression and Phenotypic Diversity in 2,311 Rapeseed Accessions*. Relevant to TE categories and genome-wide diversity.
- Official production guidance: https://research-figure-guide.nature.com/figures/preparing-figures-our-specifications/ and https://www.nature.com/ncomms/submit/how-to-submit .

For publication, follow the *actual target journal's most recent submission guidelines*. Do not claim this repository is an official Nature template.
