# TEV classification schematic

Minimal visual rule: an SV interval with **more than 80% TE-covered sequence** is classified as a **TEV**.

- The bracket identifies the complete SV interval.
- The coral portion represents TE-covered sequence.
- The dashed vertical marker indicates the 80% threshold; the illustrated TE-covered portion extends beyond it.
- The neutral segment represents the remaining sequence.
- Overlapping TE annotations should be merged (union of covered bases) before calculating the coverage fraction, so bases are not double-counted.

This is an operational overlap-based classification, not proof by itself of the molecular mechanism that generated the SV. Canvas: 1400 × 250, matching the companion TE architecture SVGs.
