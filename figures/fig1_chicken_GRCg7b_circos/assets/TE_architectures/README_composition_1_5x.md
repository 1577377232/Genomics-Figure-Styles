# Composition-specific 1.5× SVG assets

These five SVGs are composition-only variants for the V15 Circos figure. They preserve the original 1400 × 250 viewBox and the structural module geometry, but reduce the class-heading font from 58 to 39 units. When the compositor scales each SVG to 1.5× its previous display width, the module geometry and internal labels become 1.5× larger while the LINE/LTR/SINE/DNA/TEV headings remain approximately their previous rendered size.

Use these files in the R composition script:

- `TE_architectures/LINE_CR1_composition_1_5x.svg`
- `TE_architectures/LTR_ERV_composition_1_5x.svg`
- `TE_architectures/SINE_generic_composition_1_5x.svg`
- `TE_architectures/DNA_TIR_composition_1_5x.svg`
- `TE_mediated_SV/TEV_classification_composition_1_5x.svg`

The four canonical TE architecture SVGs and the canonical TEV SVG are intentionally left unchanged. The TEV composition asset is based on the V3 R2 simplified schematic: SV bracket, TE-covered interval, neutral remainder and >80% marker; no internal result arrow or separate result box.
